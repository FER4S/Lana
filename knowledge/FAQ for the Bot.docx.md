## **4\. Rescheduling & Cancellation Policy**

**Q: How do I reschedule an appointment?** A: You can reschedule through this WhatsApp chat. Just say "I want to reschedule" and I'll show you your upcoming sessions and walk you through the process. You can also reschedule through the Practice Better website or app.

**Q: How do I cancel an appointment?** A: You can cancel through this WhatsApp chat. Say "I want to cancel" and I'll guide you through the process. You can also reschedule through the Practice Better website or app.

**Q: What is the cancellation/rescheduling policy?** A: The policy is based on how much notice you provide:

* **More than 24 hours' notice:** Full refund or complimentary reschedule.  
* **Less than 24 hours' notice:** A 50% Late Cancellation Fee applies. The remaining 50% is refunded or credited.  
* **Same-day cancellation or no-show:** Non-refundable. The full session fee is charged.

**Q: Why are there cancellation fees?** A: These fees reflect the lost availability for other clients and the significant time dedicated to preparing for each individual session.

## **5\. Late Arrivals & No-Shows**

**Q: What happens if I'm late to my session?** A: A grace period is provided for each session type:

* Initial Consultation: 15-minute grace period  
* Follow-up Session: 10-minute grace period  
* Quick Call: 5-minute grace period

If you're running late, please notify Alexandra via WhatsApp during the grace period. The session may proceed, but it will still end at the originally scheduled time. If the remaining time is insufficient, a new booking at the full session rate may be required.

**Q: What happens if I don't show up (no-show)?** A: If you have not joined or contacted Alexandra by the end of the grace period, the appointment is marked as a No-Show. No-shows are strictly non-refundable and the full fee is charged.
