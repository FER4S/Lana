# Gut Repair Phases (5R Model)

> Source: `Gut Repair Phases (5R Model).docx` (Alexandra Daccache, SEED Health and Wellness Centre).
> Converted verbatim. Do not paraphrase — this file is prompt material.

**Overview:** The 5R approach (Remove, Replace, Reinoculate, Repair, Rebalance) is a phased
functional medicine framework to restore optimal gastrointestinal function. Each phase builds upon
the previous one to repair the gut, balance the microbiome, and restore immune and metabolic
homeostasis.

## PHASE 1: REMOVE (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** Eliminate sources of irritation and imbalance including pathogens, inflammatory foods, and toxins.

**Key Actions:**

Remove inflammatory foods:

- Elimination diet: Remove gluten, dairy, soy, corn, eggs, refined sugar, alcohol, caffeine.
- Consider low FODMAP or AIP if symptoms persist or SIBO is suspected.

Remove pathogens:

- Stool testing (e.g., GI-MAP, GI Effects) to identify parasites, yeast (e.g., Candida), pathogenic bacteria (e.g., H. pylori).
- Targeted antimicrobials: Botanicals (e.g., berberine, oregano oil, garlic extract, caprylic acid), or pharmaceuticals (e.g., Rifaximin for SIBO).

Remove toxins:

- Reduce chemical exposure (pesticides, plastics, NSAIDs).
- Support detox pathways (sauna, sweating, binders like activated charcoal, glutathione support).

**Duration:** 2–4 weeks depending on symptom burden and testing.

**Cautions:**

- Start slowly with antimicrobials to minimize Herxheimer reactions.
- Monitor for nutrient deficiencies during restricted diets.

## PHASE 2: REPLACE (Weeks 2–6, overlaps with Remove)

**Goal:** Support digestion by replacing missing or insufficient digestive secretions.

**Key Actions:**

Replace digestive enzymes:

- Broad-spectrum enzymes with meals (especially protease, amylase, lipase).
- Ox bile or bile salts for fat digestion (especially post-cholecystectomy).

Replace stomach acid:

- Betaine HCl with pepsin (if no gastritis, ulcers, or NSAID use).
- Lemon juice or apple cider vinegar before meals (for milder cases).

Replace nutrients:

- Replete iron, B12, magnesium, zinc, vitamin D, omega-3s as needed based on labs.

**Assessment:** Look for resolution of bloating, early satiety, reflux, constipation/diarrhea after meals.

## PHASE 3: REINOCULATE (Weeks 4–8+)

**Goal:** Restore beneficial bacteria to rebalance the microbiome.

**Key Actions:**

Probiotic supplementation:

- Multi-strain probiotics (e.g., Lactobacillus, Bifidobacterium).
- Spore-based probiotics (e.g., Bacillus coagulans) for sensitive patients or post-antimicrobials.
- Saccharomyces boulardii: especially post-antibiotics or with yeast overgrowth.

Prebiotic foods and fibers:

- Slowly introduce resistant starch, inulin, FOS, GOS, partially hydrolyzed guar gum.
- Food-based sources: artichokes, leeks, garlic, onions, green banana flour.

Fermented foods (as tolerated):

- Sauerkraut, kefir, kimchi, miso, yogurt (non-dairy if sensitive).

**Cautions:**

- Introduce fiber/prebiotics slowly to prevent bloating.
- Avoid if SIBO is active or suspected (test first).

## PHASE 4: REPAIR (Weeks 6–12+)

**Goal:** Heal and regenerate intestinal mucosa and reduce inflammation.

**Key Actions:**

Nutrients and supplements:

- L-glutamine (3–5g BID) – supports enterocyte repair.
- Zinc carnosine – enhances mucosal integrity.
- Aloe vera, marshmallow root, slippery elm – mucilaginous, soothing.
- N-acetyl glucosamine (NAG), quercetin – anti-inflammatory for mast cell or immune-related gut issues.

Antioxidants and mitochondrial support:

- Vitamin A, C, E, curcumin, omega-3 fatty acids.
- Glutathione, NAC, CoQ10 – especially in chronic fatigue or mitochondrial dysfunction.

Heal tight junctions:

- Avoid NSAIDs, alcohol, processed foods.
- Test and monitor for zonulin or LPS (lipopolysaccharide) levels if needed.

## PHASE 5: REBALANCE (Weeks 10–16+, long-term)

**Goal:** Support long-term resilience through lifestyle, stress, sleep, and circadian rhythm regulation.

**Key Actions:**

Stress management:

- Daily mindfulness practice (e.g., meditation, breathwork, vagal nerve stimulation).
- Cortisol testing (DUTCH, salivary panel) if stress-related dysbiosis is suspected.

Sleep optimization:

- Consistent circadian rhythms.
- Blue light avoidance, magnesium glycinate, calming herbs (e.g., passionflower, lemon balm).

Movement:

- Gentle daily movement (walking, yoga).
- Avoid overtraining; excessive exercise can worsen gut permeability.

Continued dietary balance:

- Mediterranean-style or personalized anti-inflammatory diet.
- Rotating foods, fiber diversity, and minimal processed foods.

Monitor and retest:

- Retest microbiome, nutrient status, and inflammation markers 3–6 months post-intervention.

## Sample 5R Timelines by Diagnosis

| Phase | IBS | SIBO | Candida | Post-Infection (e.g., post-Giardia) |
| :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: | :-: |
| REMOVE | 2–4 weeks elimination diet (low FODMAP) | 4–6 weeks antibiotics or herbal antimicrobials | 4–6 weeks antifungals + diet | 3–4 weeks removal of irritants, infections |
| REPLACE | 2 weeks digestive enzymes & bitters | 2 weeks HCl + enzymes | 2 weeks enzymes + bile support | 2 weeks digestive support |
| REINOCULATE | 4–6 weeks multi-strain probiotics | 4–8 weeks probiotics + prebiotics | 4–8 weeks probiotics, Saccharomyces boulardii | 4 weeks probiotics + fermented foods |
| REPAIR | 4–6 weeks glutamine, zinc, curcumin | 4–6 weeks gut-healing nutrients | 6 weeks gut-healing nutrients | 4–6 weeks gut lining repair |
| REBALANCE | Ongoing lifestyle changes + stress mgmt | Ongoing lifestyle + stress mgmt | Lifestyle + antifungal diet long term | Stress reduction + balanced diet |
