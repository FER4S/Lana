# Standard vs Unique Plan Logic

> Converted verbatim. Do not paraphrase — this file is prompt material.

## Standard Plans

These are templated protocols built around well-established symptom clusters or common diagnoses
(e.g. SIBO, estrogen dominance, fatigue, dysbiosis, constipation). They include:

- Pre-built supplement protocols
- Food and lifestyle recommendations
- Monitoring instructions (e.g. 2-week bowel updates)

### When to use a standard plan

Use these when:

- Patient fits a clear and common clinical picture
- Labs (if available) align with protocol targets
- Time is limited and the case is lower complexity

## Unique (Customized) Plans

These are tailored based on a combination of patient history, labs, symptoms, and obstacles.
Often require deviation from templates.

### When to use a unique plan

Use these when:

- Multiple overlapping root causes (e.g. fatigue + histamine + mold)
- Unclear diagnosis or history of failed protocols
- Patient has multiple sensitivities, severe fatigue, neurological symptoms, or surgical history
  (e.g. bowel resection)
- They're in a special population (e.g. pregnancy, pediatrics, cancer)

## Approach

Start with standard frameworks, then layer or adjust based on:

- Severity
- Individual response
- Lab anomalies
- Patient feedback and budget
