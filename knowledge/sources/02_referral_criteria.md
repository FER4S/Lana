Refer for deeper investigation when:

## Unexplained or Severe Symptoms

- Weight loss or gain without cause
- Dizziness, syncope, arrhythmia-like symptoms
- Persistent fatigue despite mitochondrial/GI support

## Symptoms Not Improving After 8–12 Weeks

- E.g., no change in constipation after full 5R plan
- Ongoing skin rashes despite gut + histamine protocols

## Red Flag Clusters

- Night sweats + weight loss + pain (consider imaging, oncology referral)
- Blood in stool, black tarry stools, anemia of unknown origin (GI referral)
- Frequent infections, persistent lymphadenopathy (immune workup)
- Unilateral headaches or neurological symptoms (neuro or imaging)

## Before Specific Interventions

- Considering DHEA → check hormones & adrenal markers
- Considering methylation support → check homocysteine, B12, MTHFR
- Recurring miscarriages → test thyroid, PCOS, progesterone, clotting factors

## When Symptoms Suggest Another Specialty

- Pelvic pain unresponsive to gut support → GYN, pelvic ultrasound
- Severe joint pain → rheumatology
- Chronic cough + GI issues → rule out GERD, pulmonary referral
