> Source: `_Treatment Plan with SEED prodcuts.docx` (Alexandra Daccache, SEED Health and Wellness Centre).
> These are the products that appear in her real treatment plans. **Drafts should name products from
> this list**, not generic ingredients ("Magnesium Citrate", not "a magnesium supplement").
> `PlanKnowledge.formulary_names()` parses the first column of every table below; anything a draft
> proposes that isn't matched here is flagged "not in your SEED formulary" rather than silently
> accepted — it is a flag, not an error, since she prescribes outside the list when a case needs it.
>
> Most items are marked "Available at SEED" in her plans. Biogena items are ordered via Amrita using
> the invite code in `08_plan_template.md`.
